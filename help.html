Rotor · Cloud backup, recovery key and other devices · Español · Português

The essentials: your watches live on your phone. If you turn on the cloud backup, they are also stored on a server and you can restore them with your recovery key. Rotor doesn’t ask for an email or an account: the key is the only thing that identifies your backup, so keep it safe.

Logging what you wear

How do I log the watch I’m wearing?

In Collection tap Put on… and choose the watch, or tap Put it on on the suggested watch or on any watch’s page. You wear one at a time: when you put on another, the previous one keeps the real hours you wore it in its history. When you take it off, tap I took it off. For other days use Another day, or tap a day in History → Calendar.

What are families?

Diver, chronograph, GMT, pilot, field, dress, sport or GADA (go anywhere, do anything). Each watch has one, chosen on its page or when you edit it; Rotor suggests one from the brand and model, but you decide. With 6 or more watches and at least two families, you can filter your collection by family.

Where your watches are stored

Where is my data?

On your phone, inside the browser that opens Rotor (usually Chrome). That is why it works offline and without an account. Uninstalling Rotor usually does not delete it, but “Clear data” in Chrome or for Rotor’s website does. To be safe, turn on the cloud backup or save a backup file now and then (Settings → Backup JSON).

The cloud backup

What is saved and when?

Your watches (with photos and wear history) and your wishlist. They are saved automatically a few seconds after each change, when you open the app and when the connection comes back. Settings → System status shows when it last happened.

Who can see my backup?

Nobody but you. Everything is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your recovery key, and that key is never sent. The server only holds ciphertext: neither Rotor nor Cloudflare can read it. For that same reason, if you lose the key and have no device left with Rotor, the backup cannot be recovered.

What happens if I turn it off?

Changes stop being uploaded. What was already in the cloud stays there until you tap “Delete from the cloud”.

Your recovery key

What is it?

A 16-character code, for example 7KQ2-M4XR-9TBW-2HNF, created when you turn the cloud on. It works like a password: whoever has it can see and delete your backup. Keys created before version 2.8 are longer and still work; “Change key” gives you a short one.

Where do I keep it?

In your password manager, or send it to yourself with the “Save…” button. It is in Settings → Cloud backup.

I think someone has seen it

Tap “Change key”: a new key is created and the backup saved with the old one is deleted. Then link your other devices again.

Using Rotor on another device

With a 6-character code (fastest)

On the device that already has your watches: Settings → Link another device. A code such as K7Q-M4X and a QR code appear.

On the new one: Settings → Use a key or code and type the code, or scan the QR code with the camera. Each code works for one device only and expires after 10 minutes: for several devices, create a code for each one. Your recovery key, on the other hand, always works on any device.

With your recovery key

On the new device: Settings → Use a key or code and type the key. Whatever was already there is combined with the backup; nothing is deleted.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app

How do I get my watches back?

On a new phone, or after clearing Chrome’s data, Rotor starts empty. To get your watches back:

  • If the cloud was on: open Rotor, tap “I already use Rotor: restore my watches” (or Settings → Use a key or code) and type your key. If you still have another device with Rotor, a linking code also works.
  • If you didn’t use the cloud: you need a backup file (Settings → Backup JSON). Restore it in Settings → Restore backup.

If the cloud was on but you lost the key and have no device left with Rotor, there is no way to recover the backup: we don’t know whose it is.

Backups (JSON)

Why do I need them if I use the cloud?

They are a file you keep, independent of the server. If you don’t use the cloud, they are the only way not to lose your collection. The app reminds you after a month without a backup.

Fill in from a link

How does it work?

Paste the link to the shop or the official site and the server downloads that public page. The app looks for the spec sheet in it (tables and lines like “Diameter: 41 mm”) and copies the data word for word; it doesn’t use artificial intelligence or guess anything. Under each detail you see the line of the page it came from, and you choose what to apply.

Some shops (Amazon, Chrono24…) block automatic downloads. In that case copy the specifications table and paste it into “Paste the spec text”: it is read on your phone and sent nowhere.

Rotor Pro

What does it include and how much is it?

Maintenance history with costs, accuracy in seconds per day, straps, collection value and an insurance inventory in PDF. It is a one-time payment in the Google Play app, no subscription. Everything free stays free.

I changed phones: do I pay again?

No. Open Rotor from Google Play with the same Google account and Pro switches on by itself. If not, Settings → Rotor Pro → “I already bought it: restore”.

Phone reminders

Settings → Phone reminders (Pro). It reminds you when the battery is due a check (2 years), when a service is due (5 years since the last one) and when a watch has spent a month in the box. Android decides when the app wakes up, so a reminder may arrive a few hours late. Everything is worked out on the phone.
