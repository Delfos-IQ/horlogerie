Privacy

Rotor (formerly Horlogerie) · Last updated: 12 October 2026 (v2.16) · Español · Português

In short: your collection is stored on your device. It only leaves it if you turn on “Cloud backup”, and then it is encrypted on your device before upload so you can use it on other devices. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no selling of data. You can delete your cloud data from inside the app.

Cloud backup (optional)

It is off on new installs. If you turn it on in Settings, your collection, photos, history and wishlist are stored on our own service hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., as data processor).

End-to-end encryption: before leaving your device, each watch, wishlist item and photo is encrypted (256-bit AES-GCM) with a key derived from your recovery key, which is never sent. The server only stores ciphertext, an account identifier derived from the key and the hash of a write token: neither we nor Cloudflare can read your collection. What the server does see is how many items you have, their size and when they change.

  • Your recovery key works like a password: whoever has it can see and delete your backup. Don’t share it. You can change it in Settings (“Change key”). As there are no accounts or email, if you lose it we cannot recover it for you.
  • Linking another device: your device encrypts the key with a 6-character code that is generated on it and never sent; the server keeps that ciphertext for 10 minutes at most and deletes it once used.
  • Deletion: Settings → “Delete from the cloud” removes your backup immediately. If you turn the backup off without deleting it, it stays stored until you delete it.
  • Technical logs: to limit abuse, the service keeps for 1 hour a counter tied to an encrypted digest (hash) of your IP address, and for 24 hours technical errors with that same digest. Never the plain IP.
  • International transfers: Cloudflare may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards of its standard contractual clauses.

Fill in from a link

If you use “Fill in from a link” with a link, that link is sent to our service on Cloudflare, which downloads the public page and returns its text to your device, where the app reads the specifications. No artificial intelligence or other external service is used, and nothing from your collection, your photos or your key is sent. The text of each page is cached for 30 days so it is not downloaded again. If you paste the spec text instead of a link, it is read on your device and never leaves it.

Purchases (Rotor Pro)

Rotor Pro is bought through Google Play: Google processes the payment and the app never sees your card details. To check the purchase, the app sends the purchase code given by Google Play to our service on Cloudflare; the service looks it up in the Google Play API, acknowledges the purchase and does not store it. Your device keeps a note that Pro is on. The policyholder name you can type when creating the insurance inventory is only written into the PDF: it is neither stored nor sent.

Phone reminders (Rotor Pro)

If you turn them on, the app prepares the list of reminders on your device (batteries, services and watches unworn for a month) and the phone itself shows them. No external notification service is used and nothing leaves the device.

Third parties

Apart from the optional features described (cloud backup, fill in from a link and the Rotor Pro purchase check), the app does not talk to external services: fonts, icons and libraries are bundled with the app. QR codes are generated on your device. There is no advertising or analytics SDK.

The Rotor website and cookies

The presentation website (rotor.pedicode-app.workers.dev) uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. The only thing it stores in your browser is that you closed the language suggestion or the browser notice («Rotor works best in Chrome»), if you do, and when (to remind you after a month). The app, at /app/ on the same site, keeps your data in your browser as explained above. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, processes technical connection data (such as the IP address) to serve it and protect it from abuse.

Backups

“Backup (JSON)” creates a file on your device with your data and photos. That file stays under your control: the app does not send it anywhere.

Your rights

You can access your data (it is in the app and in the JSON backup), correct it and delete it from inside the app. For any other request (GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability) write to the contact address. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Portugal, the CNPD; in Spain, the AEPD; in the UK, the ICO).

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.
