## What Rotor is

Rotor helps you keep track of your watch collection: which watch you are wearing, which one is next, specifications, servicing and value. It works in the browser and as an Android app.

The information it shows is a guide. Specifications are copied from the pages you choose and may contain mistakes; battery and service reminders use general intervals and are no substitute for a watchmaker's advice; values and cost per wear are calculations based on the data you enter.

## Free use

The free features need no account and no payment. We may improve or change them over time.

## Rotor Pro

- Rotor Pro is a **one-time payment** (€5.99 in the euro area; Google Play shows the price in other countries). It is not a subscription.
- It is bought inside the Android app. Payment is handled by Google Play under its own terms; refunds follow Google Play's policy, without prejudice to your statutory rights.
- The purchase is tied to your Google account and comes back when you install Rotor on another Android device with that account.
- The Pro features you buy will remain available for as long as Rotor is offered.

## Your data and the cloud backup

- Your watches are stored on your device. We recommend making backups (Settings → JSON backup).
- The cloud backup is optional. It is encrypted on your device with your recovery key, which is never sent. **If you lose the key and no device with Rotor is left, the backup cannot be recovered.**
- The cloud is provided as is and may have interruptions. If it were ever discontinued, we would announce it in the app in advance so you can export your data.
- How data is handled is explained in the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
