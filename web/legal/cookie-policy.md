The only things stored in your browser are what the app needs to work and what you decide (for example, closing a notice).</p></div>

## What is stored in your browser

Instead of cookies, Rotor uses the browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). This data is **not sent anywhere**: it stays on your device. Only the cloud copy, if you turn it on, leaves the device, and it does so encrypted (see the [Privacy policy](privacy.html)).

**In the app** (/app/):

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your collection, wishlist and photos | The app's content (watches, wear history, wishlist, photos) |
| Recovery key and cloud status | Only if you turn on the cloud copy: to encrypt and sync it |
| Preferences | The language, theme and currency you choose |
| Rotor Pro | If you bought Pro, to remember it on this device |
| Phone reminders (Pro) | The battery and service reminder plan, if you turn them on |
| Last backup and last fact | To remind you to back up and not to repeat the fact of the day |
| App files (service worker) | So the app opens offline |

**On the website**, only if you close one of these notices:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Language notice | Not to suggest another language again |
| Browser notice | Not to remind you for a month that Rotor works best in Chrome |

All of this is **strictly necessary** for the service you request or stores a decision of yours, so it needs no prior consent.

## Third parties

None inside the website or the app. Cloudflare, which hosts the website and the server, processes technical connection data (such as the IP address) to serve it and protect it from abuse, with no tracking cookies. If you buy Rotor Pro in the Android app, Google Play handles the payment under its own policy.

## How to delete it

- **From the browser:** in the site settings (padlock or «i» next to the address) → «Clear data» or «Site data».
- **From the app:** uninstalling it or clearing the site data deletes your collection from this device. Make a backup first (Settings → Export) or turn on the cloud, because it cannot be undone.
